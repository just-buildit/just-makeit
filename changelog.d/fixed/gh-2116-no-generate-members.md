- **No command writes what a `no_generate` module owns any more (gh-2116).** `jm apply` replayed the members of a `no_generate` module's objects into a scratch tree it had never scaffolded those objects in, and exited 1 on `object 'o' not found in module 'mod'`; with a `c_prefix` it then refused the author's own declaration of a property getter as a collision. Every member verb -- `jm method`, `property`, `warning`, `error`, `record`, `view`, `function` and `object --module` -- rewrote the module's hand-written C, binding, `__init__.py` and `.pyi`, `jm perf` rewrote its objects' headers, and `jm apply` patched their `step()` from an `impl`, promoted their destructor to `int` and offered their header accessor declarations. Now `apply` writes none of it and exits 0, each member verb refuses before anything is written and names the route instead -- edit the C by hand -- `jm perf` skips such an object, and `jm adopt` says why it cannot adopt one rather than that `apply` would create its fragment.
