- **An inline `string_enum:` init param whose choices mix bound and unbound constants is refused, not a traceback (gh-2744).** `--init-param 'kind:string_enum:lo,hi=lo'` read the trailing `=lo` as the constant bound to `hi`, and `lo` as the only choice without one. The reader indexed a constant that did not exist and raised `IndexError`. The flag now refuses the spec and names the unbound choice, the bound one, and the spelling of a default: `name:string_enum:lo,hi:lo`. A spec whose choices all bind a constant, or none does, is unchanged.
