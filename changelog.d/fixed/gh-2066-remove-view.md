- **`jm remove` takes out a view, and a view's own members (gh-2066).** `jm view` and the `--view` flag of `jm method`, `property`, `warning` and `error` had no undo short of editing the manifest. `jm remove view <ClassName> --object <obj>` now removes the view and its binding fragment, wherever the view's class lives, and regenerates the modules that rendered it. `--view <ClassName>` on `jm remove method|property|warning|error` removes that view's own member and leaves the parent's. Either way, the C the member bound stays in place and is named in a "delete by hand" note. `jm record`'s refusal of a type change under a view's method (gh-2055) now prints the remove-and-re-add route instead of saying there is none. Removing a read-only property no longer names a setter it never had.
